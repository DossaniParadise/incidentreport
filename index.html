Incident Report

Reporting procedure (tap to open)
  1. Immediately report any accident, incident or injury to a supervisor or manager.
  2. Determine the extent of injuries. If medical treatment is needed, call the nearest Urgent Care clinic provided by DP Management. Contact Human Resources before the employee leaves the store so an Authorization to Treat Form can be submitted.
  3. If the condition warrants, or it is after hours, call an ambulance or other emergency personnel.
  4. A statement must be completed by the customer or employee involved and by 2 eyewitnesses. If the person involved will not complete and sign, a witness completes it as closely as possible and notes that they would not sign.
  5. The manager or supervisor on duty completes the Manager Incident Report and the Investigation of Incident immediately after the incident.
  6. Photograph the area or hazard as soon as possible, with a date/time stamp if you can.
  7. If there is video monitoring, review the footage, preserve it, and be ready to submit it.
  8. Send all reports, photos and video to your Area Coach and Human Resources within 24 hours of the incident.
